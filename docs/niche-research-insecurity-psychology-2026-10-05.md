# Niche research: insecurity / social-psychology Shorts
**Date:** 2026-10-05 · **Method:** live YouTube Shorts search results and channel Shorts tabs, plus 2026 industry write-ups on the Shorts algorithm and the inauthentic-content policy (sources at the bottom).

## Why a niche change
The current fact/trivia channel plateaus at ~1k views per Short (see `shorts-performance-report-2026-09-17.md`): ~28% engaged-view rate, 42% median AVP, 0 shares across 22 videos. That is the size of YouTube's seed test pool — the Shorts are not passing it. Trivia is saturated and gives the viewer nothing to *do* (no self-check, nobody to send it to).

### Topic families with repeated breakouts
1. **Hidden signals from other people**: "signs someone secretly dislikes you" (289K–1.8M across several channels), "if a person makes direct eye contact with you…" (3.5M).
2. **"Am I attractive?" reassurance**: "4 Signs You're Super Attractive" (3.3M), "10 Clear Signs You're an Attractive Person" (389K).
3. **Tricks to be more attractive or respected**: "Tricks to Impress Almost Anyone" (70K), "4 Psychological Tricks to Attract Your Crush" (683K).

### Structure of the winners
- Second person ("you"), a self-check, an emotional payoff (reassurance or warning).
- 3–5 fast points, 30–60 s, calm authoritative voiceover.
- Ends with a self-recognition prompt ("did you notice this in yourself?") → comments.

## Findings that constrain the plan
1. **Generic listicles are saturated.** ~15 channels post near-identical titles ("5 Signs You're Actually Very Attractive"); most sit at 500–2K views — the same ceiling we have now. Being different (a sharper hook, a specific surprising point, a strong first frame) is required, not optional.
2. **The mega-hits on money and beauty come from on-camera creators** (Mark Tilbury, Petite Dressing, Victoria Pfeifer, Matt Randon). Even Academy of Wealth (547K subs, a 56M hit) now gets 200–2K per Short. → For a faceless pipeline, focus on **attraction, respect and social-signal psychology**, not money or beauty.
3. **YouTube punishes clickbait without a payoff.** Survey answers, swipe-aways and likes after the hook outweigh raw watch time; a high-pull hook followed by early drop-off now suppresses distribution. Benchmarks: <30% swipe-away in the first 2 s, >70% average viewed.
4. **Inauthentic-content policy** (renamed from "repetitious content", July 2025): templated, near-identical, mass-produced videos risk a warning → 90-day YPP suspension → removal. A January 2026 enforcement wave terminated channels with billions of views. Variety in structure, visuals and phrasing is a compliance requirement.

## Policy no-go list for this niche
- No medical, diagnostic or mental-health-condition claims ("signs you have BPD"). This keeps the existing medical exclusion.
- No sexualised visuals; no AI-generated "attractive woman" imagery.
- No miracle or money-making promises; no investment advice.
- No degrading or shaming of a gender or group. Frame things as "signals" and "habits", never "ugly" or "worthless".
